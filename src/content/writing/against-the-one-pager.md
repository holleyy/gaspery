The default shape for "I made a thing" is a landing page: a hero shot, three
feature cards, a pricing table, a call to action repeated four times.

My apps aren't products. They're tools I made for myself and left the door
open on. A landing page would be lying about what they are.

## What they get instead

A paragraph. A screenshot if it helps. A link. If you need it, you'll know
within a sentence whether it's for you — and if you don't, I haven't wasted a
scroll of your time.
