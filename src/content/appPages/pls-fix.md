pls fix. exists because I kept generating HTML decks with Claude and then opening a text editor just to nudge one color or fix a line of copy. Now I don't — it opens the deck, lets me edit it visually, and saves the change straight back to the same file.

No new format, no export step. It's still just HTML. I'm just not hand-editing it anymore.
