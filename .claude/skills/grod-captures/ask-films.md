# Film takes for the GRØD site

Short screen recordings of the Demo, for the home page, Features and Craft.
They are cut into silent loops of three to seven seconds, so each take only
has to contain the action once, cleanly. This is for the session after the
type-size work, when the hero film is being reshot anyway.

## How every take is recorded

- **The Demo, in the house look:** Riso light, Overprint D2, medium grain, echo
  on, default text size. Reduce Motion off.
- **Native pixels, nothing added.** Record the Demo window at its canonical
  size, so the window is 2000 × 1440 pixels in the file (the stills' frame,
  2224 × 1664 with the window at 112,76, is ideal, and is what the last hero
  take used). No scaling, no automatic zoom, no recorder overlay or capture
  pill, no rounded-corner or shadow effects from the recording tool.
- **The original file, uncompressed** (`.mov` as recorded). No sound is needed.
- **A second of stillness at each end.** Start recording, wait a second, do the
  action once at an unhurried pace, wait a second, stop.
- **The pointer.** Where a click drives the action, keep the pointer and move
  it deliberately. Where the scene plays by itself, park it outside the window.
- **A clean start.** Use File → Reset Demo Workspace before each take, and
  reopen the scene for a retake.
- **File names** as given below, in one folder, `~/Downloads/grod-film-takes`.

halves of the Features page's Notes claim. The fourth replaces two stills on
the home page.

## Only if the Demo can stage them

These have no scene today as far as the README says. Skip any that would need
more than a small addition.

- `processing-steps.mov`: the stamp stepping through its steps in words
  (Transcribing, Improving transcript, Identifying speakers, Summarizing,
  Structuring notes), with the dot arriving on the Summary tab.
- `bar-idle-to-recording.mov`: the recording bar going from idle to recording,
  with the Record keycap pressed. The recording scenes are presentation-only,
  so this may not be possible without opening a capture device, in which case
  leave it.
- `look-picker.mov`: the look picker stepping through three or four themes and
  two or three dock styles on the Agenda.

## Checks before handing it over

- Each file plays at native size, with the window edges sharp.
- No take shows a recorder overlay, a notification, the menu bar clock or the
  desktop.
- `hero-continuity.mov` begins and ends on the same view.
